Game Rules

Farmer Fern’s garden is in trouble! Sneaky moles keep popping out of their burrows to nibble her veggies, and she needs your help to chase them off before they ruin the harvest.

  • Tap or click a mole the moment it pops out of its hole, before it ducks back underground.
  • You have 30 seconds to scare off as many moles as you can.
  • Every mole you catch earns you 10 points.
  • Go for the High Score: Be quick, stay sharp, and see if you can protect the whole garden!
